# Premium Reference Notes — Threshold

## Jeton

Jeton uses a deliberately simple, saturated opening statement: a single field of electric vermilion, a very large white display headline, and a single call-to-action cluster. The contrast is immediate and controlled rather than busy. Its homepage moves through staged product snippets and a stepwise flow, which makes capability feel tangible without an interface becoming visually dense.

**Applicable principle:** Use one ownable, high-contrast moment for the injected event rather than painting the entire application red. Let the surrounding environment remain quiet so the event feels consequential.

## Liquid

Liquid combines an almost-black atmospheric canvas with an editorial serif headline, a restrained navigation bar, a small trust-benefit line, and a product interface shown as evidence below the message. The key premium cues are extremely spare composition, disciplined typography, deep value contrast, and the product interface carrying the visual proof.

**Applicable principle:** Present the Threshold route monitor as the product hero itself. Use a dark graphite operating surface, one high-character display face for the strategic message, a compact operational sans for numeric data, and make the synchronized liability fork the dominant evidence on screen.

## Initial Design Guardrails

Threshold will avoid generic dashboard conventions such as a left sidebar, decorative purple gradients, bloated metric cards, uniform rounded rectangles, and an indiscriminate sea of status chips. Animation should be purposeful: establish a resting operational state, then run a tightly choreographed heat-event cascade that makes driver and cargo branches escalate together before exposing their divergence at the next waypoint.

## Atoma

Atoma produces authority through a high-contrast transition from a dark, gridded visual field into a light operating surface. It lets oversized numerical objects overlap the composition while using small uppercase labels to establish a precise financial-system rhythm. Its diagrams are treated as part of the brand language, not decoration.

**Applicable principle:** Threshold should use an engineered field map rather than a generic map tile. The route should live on a subtle coordinate grid, while data labels remain compact, uppercase, and deliberate. Severity changes should recompose the map with oversized numerical evidence and branch geometry rather than extra copy.

## Topology

Topology demonstrates that a compelling technical brand can be very spare. It leans on a dark neutral ground, tightly controlled text blocks, monochrome imagery, and an abstract animated logo, giving ideas enough visual solitude to feel important.

**Applicable principle:** Introduce silence into the monitor. The view should not try to display every conceivable metric. The four waypoints, the two liability tracks, and the combined decision remain the only story; every line and label exists to reinforce that fork.

## Increase

Increase gives dense financial infrastructure a calm, human-readable form through an editorial grid, crisp black typography, a distinctive acid-green accent, and product demonstrations presented as legible, layered artifacts rather than generic cards. It switches presentation modes fluidly: code, a timeline, a ledger, and a diagram all share the same visual grammar.

**Applicable principle:** Threshold’s timeline should be engineered like an evidence record, not a stack of dashboard widgets. Shift between map, numeric readout, response label, and narrative rationale with a single underlying cadence of rules, fine lines, monospaced facts, and clear vertical progression.

## Aave

Aave demonstrates confident product storytelling by pairing open white space with controlled brand signatures, compact UI fragments, and clear conversion points. It does not bury the user in raw protocol complexity; numerical proof and real product states appear in focused, separated moments.

**Applicable principle:** The injected event should have a single readable command and an obvious visual state. Once activated, the interface explains a complex split with a short visual sequence rather than extra interactions, so judges can understand the mechanics at a glance.

## Titan

Titan’s view is direct and human: a split editorial composition, one sharply phrased headline, restrained photographic proof, then compact operational facts. It uses the page to establish credibility through proportion and specificity rather than visual spectacle.

**Applicable principle:** Build the Threshold header as a small operational masthead rather than a typical SaaS dashboard bar. Keep the first visual sentence concise, and let the product itself become the proof immediately below it.

## Motion Synthesis

The final interaction will use a **state-change choreography** rather than ornamental animation. In baseline, map routes and timeline bands rest quietly. On injection, a temperature signal travels to `wp-3`; the driver-response and cargo-response indicators fork outward in lockstep; numeric exposure values settle into their critical states; and `wp-4` resolves differently—driver returns to nominal while the cargo exposure bar retains its breach level. The reset runs that visual causality in reverse. All movement is transform/opacity based, runs within a short, staged interval, uses a deliberate spring-like easing, and is disabled under reduced-motion preferences.
