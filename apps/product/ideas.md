# Threshold — Visual Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Signal Cabinet | A dark, high-integrity control-room surface where one heat event physically branches into two accountability paths. Its tension comes from restraint, geometry, and proof rather than decorative futurism. | 0.04 |
| Cartographic Editorial | A light, print-inspired spatial narrative that treats the route as a field report and lets type, coordinates, and oversized numbers carry the drama. | 0.07 |
| Thermal Monolith | A cinematic black-and-ember environment built around a single incandescent event core, with dense technical annotations emerging from the dark. | 0.02 |

## Chosen direction — Signal Cabinet

### Design Movement

**Technical editorialism with high-integrity systems design.** The visual language borrows the calm authority of modern financial infrastructure and editorial information design: precise rules, low-noise instrument panels, and distinctive moments of strategic contrast.

### Core Principles

1. **One event, visibly bifurcated.** The map’s split marker and the paired response bands must make the shared cause and independent consequences legible before a visitor reads any paragraph.
2. **Evidence takes precedence over chrome.** Temperature, exposure, response, and confidence have more visual authority than navigation, cards, or unnecessary control elements.
3. **Tension through contrast, not clutter.** Graphite silence, bone-white evidence fields, and a restricted thermal accent system create focus. Red exists only where the event becomes consequential.
4. **Temporal causality is physical.** Motion traces the event through the route, branches it into driver/cargo responses, then lets the two paths diverge naturally at the trailing waypoint.

### Color Philosophy

The baseline is **obsidian graphite** and warm mineral white, a trustworthy, non-generic alternative to the usual fintech blue or purple. The brand signature is **Vermilion Signal** (`#FF4B2B`): a highly ownable heat color used sparingly for the injected event and breach states. Driver safety is expressed with a tempered copper-orange; cargo liability uses a saturated red vermilion, so their relationship is visible without needing a legend. Quiet cyan-gray acts only as low-severity infrastructure.

### Layout Paradigm

The screen behaves like an **instrument plate** rather than a dashboard. A narrow masthead establishes the route and event command. The hero is an asymmetric two-column field: a large cartographic monitoring surface on the left and a vertical decision rail on the right. Below it, an evidence ledger stretches horizontally as a time-based record, with each waypoint becoming a wide editorial entry rather than a tiny grid card. On smaller screens, the route stays first and the decision rail moves immediately below it.

### Signature Elements

1. **The Split Beacon:** each waypoint marker contains a divided core—upper driver response and lower cargo response—surrounded by two independent ring arcs.
2. **Fork Geometry:** at the active heat event, a line visibly branches from the same point into separate driver and cargo rails, then reaches an intentionally unequal resolution at the next waypoint.
3. **Thermal Readout:** large, tightly tracked temperature figures interrupt the map surface at key events, like a field instrument rather than a chart label.

### Interaction Philosophy

The interface has one deliberate action: a weighted **Inject heat event** control. It feels like inserting evidence into a live operating record. Hover states lightly reveal structural rules and labels; they do not add novelty. Every interactive object explains a meaningful state change, and all important status is also communicated in text for accessibility.

### Animation

Baseline elements enter in a subtle stagger (30–60ms), moving only 6–10px with opacity. The injection sequence lasts roughly 900ms: the source signal ignites, travels to `wp-3`, bifurcates into driver/cargo arcs, then the rails resolve in staggered response blocks. Values use a quick count transition and the `wp-4` asymmetry settles last, emphasizing that cargo damage persists. On reset, the network retracts quickly but causally. Hover and press treatments stay below 180ms. All nonessential animation is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

**DM Mono** is the operational voice: coordinates, field labels, readings, and decision metadata. **DM Sans** is the human-readable product voice: response titles, narrative rationales, and controls. Display emphasis uses **Instrument Serif** with modest optical size, reserved only for the headline and the active temperature. Hierarchy is defined by abrupt scale changes rather than numerous weights: 11px uppercase labels, 14–16px data, 22–26px response titles, and a 56–76px display cue.

### Brand Essence

**Threshold turns one heat event into two immediate, correlated liability responses for temperature-controlled freight operators.**

**Personality:** deliberate, forensic, decisive.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are declarative and technical without sounding bureaucratic. CTAs name the outcome rather than using generic onboarding language. Microcopy recognizes uncertainty honestly.

> “One heat event. Two liabilities, resolved in context.”

> “Inject the route event”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a compact **threshold gate**: two offset rectangular apertures linked by a single diagonal signal line, suggesting a crossing that triggers a split decision. It is rendered as a bold white or graphite symbol without a letterform; the wordmark uses widened DM Sans capitals with an interrupted crossbar in the final `H`.

### Signature Brand Color

**Vermilion Signal — `#FF4B2B`**

## Style Decisions

- The product is a **single-screen evidence interface**, not a SaaS landing page or generic analytics dashboard.
- The map is a generated cartographic field and data instrument, never a stock Google map or a conventional tiled basemap.
- Rounded rectangles are used only as small field artifacts; structural surfaces use rules, not pill cards.
- No gradients, no neon, no glassmorphism, no generic dashboard sidebar, and no decorative 3D objects.
- The dormant route must preview its driver/cargo bifurcation before an event is injected; injection increases evidence intensity rather than revealing the concept for the first time.
- Warm mineral-white evidence fields are reserved for the observed temperature and the event record, establishing a clear hierarchy over the graphite operating surface.
- Driver safety remains copper-orange and cargo liability remains vermilion across every split beacon, rail, and ledger outcome.
- The opening hero previews the **shared-event-to-two-liability split** immediately; the product proof intensifies the same visual grammar rather than introducing it later.
- Instrument Serif is confined to numerical readings and tightly restrained editorial emphasis. Main proposition headlines stay declarative, legible, and operational.
- Vermilion Signal is exclusive to injected heat events, breach/cargo-liability states, and the direct event action. Copper, mineral white, graphite, and cyan-gray carry non-critical emphasis.

## Landing Page Narrative Extension

The landing page tells the Threshold story as a progression from **exposure** to **correlation** to **proof**. It keeps the operational interface as the visual hero, rather than hiding it behind product-marketing abstractions.

| Sequence | Role in the story | Signal Cabinet expression |
|---|---|---|
| Opening masthead | Establishes the thesis: one heat event creates two independent liabilities. | Oversized editorial statement above a compact field-brief summary and one decisive action. |
| Product proof | Lets the existing route monitor earn belief immediately. | The active cartographic instrument surface is embedded as a framed product artifact. |
| The blind spot | Explains why disconnected driver and cargo workflows lose the shared event. | A restrained fork diagram contrasts one event with two disconnected review paths. |
| The Threshold method | Makes the product mechanics understandable in three forensic steps. | Sequentially numbered evidence bands with paired copper/vermilion rules. |
| Decision integrity | Emphasizes that correlation does not mean forced agreement. | The persistent cargo breach and lower-confidence split become a full-width evidence moment. |
| Closing action | Invites a serious operational conversation without generic SaaS urgency. | A mineral-white contact panel interrupts graphite silence, with compact trust signals. |

Landing motion preserves the existing rule: movement is an explanation of causality. Sections reveal on approach with restrained opacity and translation; the fork diagram traces its two branches only once; and the dashboard remains interactive as the product proof.

## Editorial Footer Extension

The footer will adopt the useful structural lesson of Browserbase’s closing treatment—**high information density, generous type scale, clearly grouped utility navigation, and a decisive brand close**—without borrowing its copy or visual identity. Threshold’s version is a three-part field registry: a large wordmark and route-intelligence statement; four compact operational navigation groups; then a legal/status strip. Thin graphite rules organize the information. Copper carries standard operating cues and Vermilion appears only in the small signal mark and current event indicator.

## Information Pages and Asset Pack

The **Threshold Gate Mark** is the canonical transparent PNG asset and the sole favicon source. The asset pack will expose the original mark and a favicon-ready PNG through the documentation page, preserving a single mark rather than creating inconsistent illustrations. The Docs page will act as a compact implementation field guide. Legal and Privacy pages will be plainly marked as pre-production policy drafts, with product-specific scope and a clear requirement for counsel review before use. All pages share a lean technical header and the editorial field-registry footer so they read as one product system rather than disconnected policy templates.

## Copy Clarity Standard

Landing-page copy should state the practical outcome before using conceptual language. Prefer **“heat event,” “driver risk,” “cargo risk,” “clear action,”** and **“one record”** over internal terms such as “liability surface,” “correlated decision,” or “operating truth.” Keep the visual system forensic and premium, but make every major statement understandable on its first read.

## Conversion Proof and Contact Flow

The closing action should make a measurable-sounding but non-fabricated promise: **“Use one shared route record to reduce duplicate reviews and make the next call sooner.”** The contact flow asks only what helps prepare that conversation: name, work email, cargo type, primary problem, and fleet or program size. It opens a prefilled email draft rather than claiming to submit data to an unconfigured service, so the interaction stays honest in the static product build.

## Authenticated Product Shell

The complete app uses the existing **Signal Cabinet** system as a denser field console: charcoal rails, mineral evidence planes, sparse vermilion breach indicators, and copper driver-safety signals. One custom sign-in path leads to an organisation selection gate, then into a role-aware shell. The demo role selector is intentionally visible as an integration seam, not disguised as authentication; it lets the user inspect the dispatcher, compliance, driver, and admin information architecture before replacing it with Clerk organisation metadata.

The screen hierarchy follows task gravity rather than generic dashboard convention. Dispatch starts with routes, Compliance starts with the audit timeline, Drivers start with their assigned routes, and Admin starts with organisation configuration. The PHX — 01 route detail retains the only demo injector and makes the one-event/two-action evidence fork visible without adding superfluous controls.

## Style Decisions

- App and authentication headlines use a firmer operational DM Sans display voice; Instrument Serif remains reserved for observed temperature, confidence figures, and tightly controlled evidence numerals.
- Each major app surface uses paired copper and vermilion lines, a split beacon, or fork geometry before relying on explanatory copy to explain the driver/cargo divergence.
- The PHX — 01 heat-event injector is the only high-consequence Vermilion Signal action. Vermilion is not used as a general purpose UI button.
- The split audit case uses a mineral-white evidence interruption so important disagreement becomes a documentable review moment rather than another dark dashboard card.
